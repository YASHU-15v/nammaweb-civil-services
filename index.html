A mentor for every answer you write, not just every doubt you raise

Most coaching gives you a mentor for an hour a week. nammaweb's certified AI mentors review your work every day — mains answers, ethics case studies, interview responses — and a human subject expert signs off on your progress at each milestone.

  • Every AI mentor is trained and certified against nammaweb's KAS and UPSC CSE answer-evaluation framework before it works with a single student.
  • Daily answer-writing feedback on structure, argument and word economy — usually within a few hours, not a week.
  • A human faculty reviewer audits your AI mentor's feedback at every 15-day checkpoint.
  • Current affairs are summarised daily and linked back to your syllabus, so revision stays connected to the news cycle.
